Simple, transparent match fees

LocumLink is free for locum physicians. Hosts pay $125 for one half-day or $250 for more than one half-day per confirmed match. A half-day is 3.75 hours, in line with the GP Locum Programme guide. Two half-day blocks make a full day.

* Match example: Each confirmed locum on a posting counts as a separate match. If the same locum covers Monday morning and Wednesday afternoon on one posting, that's one $250 match. If two different locums cover those blocks, that's two $125 matches.
